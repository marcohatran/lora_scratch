In practice

Sensible starting hyperparameters

ParameterStart withWhy
r8–16Small data needs little capacity; 32–64 for hard tasks
lora_alphar or 2rKeep α / r fixed when you change r
target_modulesall linear layersBetter quality than attention q, v only
learning rate1e-4 – 2e-4About 10× higher than full fine-tuning
lora_dropout0 – 0.1Raise it when data is small
epochs1–3More epochs overfit quickly
These are defaults for 7B-class models with peft. For Llama-style models, all linear layers means q, k, v, o, gate, up and down projections; peft accepts the shortcut all-linear. Always validate on held-out data.
