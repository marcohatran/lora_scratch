01 · What is LoRA fine-tuning

Fine-tuning adapts a pre-trained model

Pre-training

Trillions of tokens. Learns general language. Months of compute — only large labs do it.

Fine-tuning

A small, specialised dataset. Learns your task, format or style. Continues training the same weights.

Wnew = Wold + ΔW

Every layer of a neural network is a weight matrix W. Fine-tuning learns the change ΔW.

A model is a stack of weight matrices. Pre-training sets them; fine-tuning nudges them for a specific job. The question LoRA answers: how do we learn ΔW cheaply?
