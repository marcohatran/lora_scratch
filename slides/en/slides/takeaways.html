Takeaways

LoRA in four lines

1

Freeze W₀ and learn ΔW = (α/r)·BA with a small rank r.

2

Train about 0.1–1% of the parameters; adapters are megabytes.

3

Start with B = 0; merge after training for zero extra latency.

4

It fits in 15 lines of PyTorch; peft does the same thing.

Notebook: lora_finetune_scratch_en.ipynb · Paper: arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685 · Docs: huggingface.co/docs/peft

If you remember one thing: LoRA learns a low-rank correction next to a frozen model. Next steps: run the notebook, then try the exercises — change r, add more target layers, and train two adapters on one base model.
