QLoRA: fine-tune big models on one GPU

W₀ is frozen, so it does not need full precision: store it in 4-bit (NF4) and train A and B in bf16. Memory for an 8B model, before activations:

Full fine-tuning (fp32 + Adam)

~128 GB

LoRA, base model in fp16

~16 GB

QLoRA, base model in 4-bit

~5 GB

QLoRA: Dettmers et al., 2023. Needs an NVIDIA GPU and the bitsandbytes library. Bars are drawn to scale.

QLoRA is the reason people can fine-tune 7B to 70B models on a single GPU. The trick only works because of LoRA: the big matrix is frozen, so it can be compressed, while the small trainable matrices stay in higher precision.
