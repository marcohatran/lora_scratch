02 · How LoRA works

Low-rank: a big matrix from two thin ones

ΔW d × d

=

B d × r

×

A r × d

With d = 4096 and r = 8:

16,777,216 → 65,536 numbers

256× fewer

The bet: the change ΔW a new task needs has a low “intrinsic rank” — the model only has to move in a few directions.

A matrix of rank r can always be written as a tall d-by-r matrix times a wide r-by-d matrix. The LoRA paper showed that ranks of 1 to 8 were often enough even for GPT-3 175B. So instead of learning ΔW directly, we learn B and A.
