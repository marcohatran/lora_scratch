In practice

Four common mistakes

Forgetting to freeze

You are secretly doing full fine-tuning. Always check print_trainable_parameters().

Learning rate too low

A full fine-tuning rate like 2e-5 barely moves LoRA. Start around 1e-4 to 2e-4.

Wrong chat template

Instruct models expect their own conversation format. Use tokenizer.apply_chat_template.

Expecting new knowledge

LoRA is best at format, style and tasks. For large bodies of facts, combine it with RAG.

Two more from the notebook: mask the prompt tokens in the loss, and keep a validation set, because tiny datasets overfit to zero training loss. With QLoRA, reload the base model in fp16 or bf16 before merging.
